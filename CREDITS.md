## Models and data

- **Parakeet** speech models by NVIDIA (CC-BY-4.0), run on the Mac through FluidAudio. They are downloaded by the user from Hugging Face the first time they are wanted.
- **Weather** data from [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com) (CC-BY-4.0); the city is found with [ipwho.is](https://ipwho.is).
- **Sounds:** the dictation click sounds are by cabled_mess (CC0) and the Talkify team; the "Plunger pop" sound is by lloydevans09 on Freesound (CC-BY-NC 4.0), used for non-commercial purposes only.
